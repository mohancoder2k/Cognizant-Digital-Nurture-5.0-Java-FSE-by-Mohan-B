Community Bulletin Board

The Chennai city council will be conducting free health check-up camps at all ward offices every Saturday in January. Residents are requested to carry their Aadhaar card and previous medical records if available.

The Thiruvalluvar Nagar park renovation is complete. The park now features a new jogging track, children's play area, and senior citizen rest zone. It will be inaugurated by the ward councillor this Sunday at 9 AM.

Water supply will be disrupted in T. Nagar, Kodambakkam, and Vadapalani areas on Thursday from 6 AM to 2 PM due to pipeline maintenance work by the Metro Water Board. Residents are advised to store water in advance.

The annual Pongal drawing competition for school children will be held at the community hall on January 12. Registration forms are available at the ward office. Last date for submission is January 10.
